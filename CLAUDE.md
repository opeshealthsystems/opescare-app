# OpesCare Finder — patient mobile app

The **OpesCare Finder** patient mobile app (Expo / React Native): drug & blood
finder, appointment booking, facility directory, and portable **Health ID**.
Forked from the OpesCare platform patient app and pointed at the Finder backend
at **finder.opescare.cloud** (`EXPO_PUBLIC_API_BASE_URL`).
